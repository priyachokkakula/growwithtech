About GrowWithTech

Software for businesses that don't have an IT department

We build practical tools and set them up ourselves — so growing businesses get enterprise-grade systems without needing to hire a tech team.

Our story

Why we built GrowWithTech

Most business software is built for enterprises with dedicated IT staff, or it's so simple it falls apart once a company grows past a handful of employees. Small and mid-sized businesses end up stuck between spreadsheets and tools they don't have the resources to properly set up.

GrowWithTech was built to close that gap: CRM, billing, and project management software that's powerful enough to run a real business, backed by a team that implements it for you instead of leaving you to figure it out alone.

Who we work with

  • Small and mid-sized service businesses
  • Agencies and consultancies
  • Growing teams outgrowing spreadsheets
  • Businesses without a dedicated IT team
